Our Featured Adventures

Trail of Fall

The Trail of Fall is an exciting rafting experience created for adventurers who want to experience fast moving water and challenging rapids. Our experienced guides provide safety instructions and help the group work together throughout the journey.

This trip is ideal for groups looking for excitement, teamwork, and beautiful river scenery.

Difficulty: Intermediate

Trail of Courage

Trail of Courage is designed for visitors who are ready for a more challenging river experience. Participants will navigate exciting rapids while learning how to work as a team under the guidance of our professional river guides.

Expect an energetic adventure, spectacular scenery, and plenty of opportunities to create unforgettable memories.

Difficulty: Advanced

Half Day Adventure

The Half Day Adventure is a great introduction to white water rafting. It offers a combination of calm river sections, exciting rapids, and scenic views without requiring a full day on the river.

This adventure is a great option for families, beginners, and visitors with limited time.

Difficulty: Beginner

River Adventure

River Adventure combines beautiful scenery with exciting sections of white water. Guests can enjoy the natural environment while working together with their rafting team.

It is an excellent choice for groups looking for a balanced experience between relaxation and adventure.

Difficulty: Intermediate
